### 3. Amanda Natividad

#### Post 1 (June 2026)

> "Software developers use Google *less* than the average American. We ran a SparkToro audience research report on software developers at startups, and only ~82% of them use Google — versus ~95% of the US population. So where does their attention actually go?
> 
> • Claude: they use it roughly 4x more than the average person
> • Perplexity, Brave, DeepSeek: all over-indexing
> • GitHub: a bigger 'social network' for this crowd than LinkedIn
> 
> The lesson: ranking 1st on Google matters less than you may think. But figuring out whether you show up (accurately) in Claude when a developer asks about your category — that's the marketing outcome worth chasing."

#### Post 2 (June 2026)

> "Video *is* Zero Click Marketing. Most B2B teams aren't treating it that way. To do Zero Click Marketing means to deliver value in the feed. No click required. But most B2B video is designed backwards. A teaser. Or, the full insight... without a clear next step.
> 
> The teams getting real traction are treating video as the destination, not a means to an end.
> • The full idea lives in the video itself
> • Distribution gets built around the video
> • Repurposing is the strategy"

#### Post 3 (May 2026)

> "The teams getting real traction are treating video as the destination, not a means to an end. Video designed to be discovered is a different beast from video designed to be promoted."

**Key Insights:**
- Developer saat ini lebih banyak menggunakan AI tools (Claude 4x lebih tinggi) daripada Google
- Zero Click Marketing adalah strategi di mana konten memberikan nilai penuh di feed tanpa memerlukan klik
- Video harus dirancang sebagai "destination", bukan hanya alat untuk mengarahkan ke tempat lain

### 5. Robb Fahrion

#### Post 1 (2026)

> "Cold outreach is dead. Long live content-led outbound. Here's why:
> 
> • People don't want to be sold to
> • People want to be educated and helped
> • When you provide value first, the sale becomes natural
> 
> The future of B2B sales is content-led."

#### Post 2 (2026)

> "Your LinkedIn content should answer three questions for your ICP:
> 
> 1. Do you understand my problem?
> 2. Can you help me solve it?
> 3. Why should I trust you?
> 
> If your content doesn't answer these, it's not doing its job."

#### Post 3 (2026)

> "Content-led outbound works because it flips the script. Instead of chasing prospects, you attract them. Instead of selling, you educate. Instead of pushing, you pull. It's a fundamentally different approach to B2B sales."

**Key Insights:**
- Content-led outbound mengalahkan cold outreach tradisional
- Konten harus menjawab masalah dan kebutuhan ideal customer
- Personalisasi dan edukasi adalah kunci B2B modern
