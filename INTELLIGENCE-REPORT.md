## 4. KEY FINDINGS

### Finding 1: Content is a System, Not an Output
Top creators operate **structured content engines**. Consistency comes from process, not willpower.

### Finding 2: Hook Performance Drives Engagement
The first 1-2 lines determine **70-80% of a post's engagement**.

**Top 5 Hook Types:**

| Rank | Hook Type | Example | Effectiveness |
|------|-----------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Contrarian | "Most founders are wrong about LinkedIn..." | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 2 | Curiosity Gap | "I almost quit content marketing... here's why" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 3 | Personal Frame | "My biggest failure taught me this..." | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 4 | Data-Driven | "Only 3% of posts get 1000+ likes" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 5 | Question-Based | "What if you stopped posting for 30 days?" | ⭐⭐⭐ |

### Finding 3: Distribution is a Growth Lever
Content performance is strongly influenced by deliberate distribution behavior:
- Strategic commenting cadence
- Network amplification
- Consistent engagement velocity

### Finding 4: Founder-Led Content Outperforms Brand
Personal identity-driven content generates **higher trust and engagement** compared to company brand pages.

## 6. CONTENT FORMAT ANALYSIS

| Format | Usage | Pros | Cons |
|--------|-------|------|------|
| **Carousel** | 40% | In-depth, visual, saveable | Time-consuming |
| **Text Thread** | 35% | Fast, personal, easy | Less visual |
| **Image + Text** | 20% | Visual hook, simple | Limited engagement |
| **Video** | 5% | Personal, strong connection | Hard to edit |

### Ideal Content Length

| Format | Ideal Length |
|--------|--------------|
| Thread | 5-15 paragraphs |
| Carousel | 5-10 slides |
| Text-only | 200-500 words |
